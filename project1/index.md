<h1>HW 1: Unity First Scene</h1>
<img src="hw1-scene-final.jpg">
<p>My scene is an open field with a farmer whistling while a bomb close by is about to explode.<br>
I made the music note with two squashed cylinders for the notes and three cubes for the stems.<br>
For the farmer I made a capsule body with sphere eyes, and a flattened spheres for the hat.<br>
Lastly, the bomb used a sphere, a cylinder, and a cube, with particle effects to show the fuse being lit.</p>
<p>Navigating the menus for materials was a bit of a challenge at first, but making them was fun.<br>
I also learned that splitting or cutting shapes requires a bit of external software to do, so I didn't<br>
do it for the time being.</p>
