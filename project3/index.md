<h1>HW 3: My First XR Experience</h1>
<a href="https://jmu.instructuremedia.com/embed/74cd763e-f581-4a57-bb69-6a914e10ddf0">My First XR Experience</a>
<p>This scene takes place somewhere in the mountains and is meant to convey the story of using explosives to mine for gold. You walk through the process of taking your explosive, putting it in the desired area, and getting some distance before blowing up the mountain and finding your reward inside. Three key takeaways I got from this project were:</p>
<ol>
    <li>Make sure your methods start with a capital letter in C#, otherwise they won't work and you'll be clueless as to why.</li>
    <li>Unity lets you attach multiple scripts to an object. That's really useful.</li>
    <li>When using sound effects, I find it easier to attach the sound to the main object and then the script to the object's trigger. Keeping them separate but close streamlines the linking process for me.</li>
</ol>
<p>The hardest part about this project was thinking of how to script out a cohesive scene that made sense narrative-wise. It's easy to make something that could be seen as confusing or guide the player off course, so finding a way to streamline the path was important. I also learned that I like to be particular about how things look and sound when designing, so thinking about how the scene should be set up and executed is something I will be able to hold onto for future projects.</p>
